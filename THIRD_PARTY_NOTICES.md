## Path of Building Community

Passive tree data (`TreeData/*/tree.lua`) and gem data (`Data/Gems.lua`) are **not** included in this
repository. They are read at runtime from the user's local
[Path of Building Community](https://github.com/PathOfBuildingCommunity/PathOfBuilding) installation (MIT License).
The orbit-angle tables in `server.py` follow PoB's `PassiveTree.lua`.
